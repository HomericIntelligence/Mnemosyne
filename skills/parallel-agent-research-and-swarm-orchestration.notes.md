## Research evidence rules

- Cite authoritative sources for baseline dimensions and empirical claims.
- Label arithmetic as derived and show inputs/formula/units.
- Separate correctness findings from novelty or product judgments.
- Report contradictions between specialists; do not silently choose one.
- Structural validity failures can cap a grade regardless of average score.

## Evidence boundary

The campaigns validate the workflow shape at their recorded scales. Agent roles, model choices,
concurrency, grading thresholds, and API capacity are not universal parameters.
